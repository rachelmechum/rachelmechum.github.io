Research

Binary black holes, waveform inference, and formation environments.

Waveform systematics

I use RIFT to study how waveform models affect the masses, spins, and other properties inferred from unequal-mass binary black hole mergers.

The mass ratio is q = m₂/m₁, where m₁ is the larger mass. The waveform comparison focuses on how model choice changes the inferred parameters.

Black holes in AGN disks

I use McFACTS to study how binary black holes form in AGN disks and embedded star clusters. I also look at whether we could detect the resulting mergers.
