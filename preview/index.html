Research

I study binary black hole mergers with gravitational-wave data and population models.

Waveform systematics

I use RIFT to study how waveform models affect the masses and spins inferred from unequal-mass binary black hole mergers.

The mass ratio is q = m₂/m₁, where m₁ is the larger mass.

Try the q = m₂/m₁ waveform demo ↗

Black holes in AGN disks

With McFACTS, I study how binary black holes form in AGN disks and embedded star clusters, and whether their mergers could be detected.
