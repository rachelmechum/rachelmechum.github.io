What a mass ratio looks like

SEOBNRv4 · m₁ = 30 M☉

The primary sits close to the center; the lighter one swings wide.

Primary 30 M☉ Secondary q × 30 M☉

peak |h|

This is a zero-spin, face-on SEOBNRv4 waveform at 400 Mpc. The primary mass is 30 M☉, and the secondary mass is q × 30 M☉. It runs from 20 Hz to merger, with every q on the same amplitude scale. The orbit is schematic and keeps the separation fixed. Detector noise is not included. I generated the waveform with LALSuite using SEOBNRv4, described by Bohé et al. 2017. RIFT uses the same waveform approximants. The masses and distance are illustrative, not from a specific event.

Black holes in AGN disks

I use population synthesis to study how binary black holes form in AGN disks and embedded star clusters, and whether we can detect their mergers.
